Cinematic photorealistic style.
Dramatic, high-contrast lighting with rich shadows.
Ultra-detailed environment, 8K quality.
No text overlays, watermarks, or logos in the image.
No extra human figures unless the script explicitly describes people.
Atmospheric depth-of-field, realistic textures.
Color palette: deep blues, warm ambers, and muted earth tones.
Wide-angle establishing shots where appropriate.
Mood: mysterious, immersive, story-driven.
Avoid cartoon or anime aesthetics.
