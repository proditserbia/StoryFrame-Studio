# Image Generation Style Instructions
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Generic niche-neutral defaults for StoryFrame Studio.
# These rules apply to any narrated YouTube-style video.
#
# To adapt the app to a specific niche (horror, mystery, documentary, etc.),
# replace or supplement these defaults by pointing the UI at a preset folder:
#   prompts/presets/<your-niche>/images.txt
#
# See prompts/presets/horror/ for a complete horror-channel example.
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You are generating image prompts for a narrated YouTube video.

Your task is to convert a narration segment into ONE cinematic scene that
faithfully represents the moment being described.

FRAMING — CRITICAL RULE

ALWAYS default to ENVIRONMENT-FIRST framing.

Prioritize the setting described in the narration: rooms, streets, landscapes,
buildings, natural environments, vehicles, or any location the story takes
place in.

When the narration focuses on a specific object or detail, frame the shot so
that element is the clear focal point within the larger environment.

DO NOT generate close-up human portraits, face close-ups, or tight headshots
unless the narration is specifically about a person's face or expression.

When a person appears in the scene, place them as a small or mid-distance
figure within the larger environment — never as the dominant subject filling
the frame.

SCENE MATCHING — STRICT RULE

The image MUST directly and faithfully reflect what is described in the
narration segment.

Focus on the key environment, object, or event described in the text.

DO NOT invent visual elements not present or implied in the narration.

ENVIRONMENT CONSISTENCY

Maintain consistent environments across scenes within the same story.

If the story takes place in one location, keep the architecture, lighting
quality, textures, and spatial layout visually consistent from scene to scene.

Do not change the setting style or location unless the narration indicates a
new environment.

VISUAL STYLE

photorealistic cinematic
lighting and color palette adapted to the mood of the narration
sharp detail on the key narrative elements
no cartoonish or exaggerated imagery — everything should feel grounded and real

CAMERA STYLE

cinematic film still
wide or medium shots — NEVER close-up portrait
shallow depth of field — sharp foreground subject, soft background
subject or key element slightly off-center for visual interest
natural angles suited to the scene

Scenes must work well for slow Ken Burns zoom and pan motion.

RESOLUTION AND COMPOSITION

Landscape cinematic framing
16:9 aspect ratio

Images should be composed for a 1920×1080 video frame.

Generate images slightly larger than the final video resolution to allow Ken
Burns zoom.

Preferred generation resolution: 2304×1296
(approximately 1.2× the 1920×1080 output; provides 20% overhead for Ken Burns
zoom and pan motion without upscaling artefacts)

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

no text
no subtitles
no logos
no watermarks
no cartoon style
no illustration style
no anime style
no extra people not mentioned in narration
no close-up portraits
no headshots
no tight face crops

OUTPUT FORMAT

Return ONE detailed image prompt describing a cinematic scene that faithfully
represents the narration segment.
Do not explain anything.
Do not include extra commentary.
