# Consistency Anchors
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Visual anchors for domestic suburban horror stories.
# These are injected into every image prompt to keep recurring environments,
# objects, and characters visually consistent across all generated scenes.
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# --- House exterior ---
A small, modest single-storey house painted pale blue with white trim.
Sits on a quiet residential corner lot. Slightly worn and weathered but
not abandoned — a recently occupied home. Small front yard, simple porch,
ordinary suburban neighborhood. Overcast sky or dusk lighting.

# --- Interior — general ---
Interiors are modest and lived-in: plain painted walls, basic furniture,
low ceilings. Not a grand or stylish home — the kind of small affordable
house you move into after a breakup. Slightly dated kitchen, narrow hallway,
compact rooms. Everything is real and ordinary, which makes wrongness
more unsettling.

# --- Hallway and attic hatch ---
A narrow interior hallway with a low ceiling. In the ceiling is a square
plywood attic hatch set into a plain frame, with a short pull-string
dangling from it. The hatch should look flush when closed, but when
displaced, a thin line of shadow is visible along one edge where it has
been nudged from above. This is the central horror element — keep its
appearance identical in every scene where it appears.

# --- Kitchen ---
A modest galley-style kitchen with an older white refrigerator, laminate
countertops, and basic cabinetry. A squeaky floorboard near the entrance.
Overhead fluorescent or single-bulb lighting. Personal items like a coffee
mug and phone charger on the counter.

# --- Colour palette ---
Muted, desaturated tones: faded blues, warm yellows from household bulbs,
grey-blue shadows at night. Deep blacks in hallway ends and ceiling corners.
No bright or saturated colors. Daytime scenes are overcast and flat.
Nighttime scenes are lit only by practical household sources — a bedside
lamp, a hallway bulb, moonlight through blinds.

# --- Character ---
When the main character appears, show them as a mid-distance or partial
figure — seen from behind, standing in a doorway, or as a silhouette
at the end of a hallway. Average build, casual home clothing. Never show
a clear face. Convey isolation and vulnerability through body language
and scale within the larger domestic space.
