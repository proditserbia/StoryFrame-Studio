# Consistency Anchors
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Generic niche-neutral consistency anchors for StoryFrame Studio.
# These rules help maintain a coherent visual language across all generated
# scenes in a video, regardless of the niche or story type.
#
# For niche-specific anchors (e.g. horror atmosphere, documentary neutrality),
# see the preset folders under prompts/presets/.
# ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# --- Visual continuity ---
Maintain consistent lighting quality, color mood, and framing style across all
scenes. When transitioning between segments, preserve the established visual
tone unless the narration explicitly signals a change of setting or time.

# --- Character depiction ---
When characters appear, depict them consistently across scenes in terms of
clothing, build, and overall appearance. Use mid-distance framing or partial
views rather than tight close-ups to preserve visual storytelling flow.
Convey emotion through body language and posture rather than facial close-ups.

# --- Environmental detail ---
Keep the level of environmental detail consistent. Spaces should feel grounded
and real, with textures, surfaces, and props that match the tone and period
described in the narration. Recurring locations should look recognizably the
same each time they appear.

# --- Compositional approach ---
Use consistent compositional techniques throughout: similar depth of field,
similar subject positioning relative to the frame, and consistent use of
negative space that supports the narrative mood. Subject placement should
remain off-centre to create visual interest without disrupting continuity.
